Academic Overview

The Department of Computer Science at PDUAM, Amjonga offers an engaging and innovative curriculum designed to prepare students for excellence in technology, research, and industry. The academic programs emphasize both theoretical knowledge and practical skills essential in the modern world of computing.

Programs Offered

  • Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Computer Science
  • Skill Development & Add-on Courses
  • Certificate Programs in Emerging Technologies

Curriculum Highlights

  • 🖥️ Programming Fundamentals (C, C++, Java, Python)
  • 🧠 Data Structures and Algorithms
  • 🌐 Web Development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
  • 💾 Database Management Systems (MySQL)
  • ⚙️ Operating Systems and Computer Networks
  • 🤖 Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (Introductory)
  • 📊 Software Engineering and Project Work

Laboratory Facilities

The department is equipped with modern computer labs featuring updated hardware and software resources. Students gain hands-on experience through lab sessions, workshops, and projects under the guidance of experienced faculty members.

Evaluation System

Evaluation is conducted through continuous assessment, internal examinations, assignments, and end-semester exams as per Gauhati University guidelines. Attendance, class participation, and project performance are integral to the grading system.
